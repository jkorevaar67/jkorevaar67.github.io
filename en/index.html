2.1 · Operations

Use the correct order of operations:

1. Brackets first. 2. Multiplication and division, from left to right. 3. Addition and subtraction, from left to right.

8 + 3 × 4 = 20

Multiply first: 3 × 4 = 12. Then add 8 + 12 = 20.

(8 + 3) × 4 = 44

Brackets first: 8 + 3 = 11. Then 11 × 4 = 44.
