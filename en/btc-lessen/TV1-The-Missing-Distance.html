The Missing Distance

Work in pairs. Use a number line. Prove your answer.

How long is the route?

The exact distance is a whole number of metres. The route app displays 4.7 km, rounded to one decimal place.

Find the shortest and longest possible distances in metres. Show why one metre less than your minimum or one metre more than your maximum changes the display.

Hint. Mark the halfway points between 4.6 and 4.7, and between 4.7 and 4.8. What happens at each halfway point?
4 650 to 4 749 m. The value 4 649 m rounds to 4.6 km. The value 4 750 m rounds to 4.8 km.
0–4 min. Laat beiden eerst een grens kiezen. Vraag daarna wie kan verdedigen of precies 4.750 km meetelt.

Two displays, one exact distance

The app shows 4.7 km to one decimal place. A route planner rounds the same exact distance to the nearest ten metres and shows 4 650 m.

Find every exact distance that fits both displays. Order them from shortest to longest. Write each in metres and kilometres. Prove your list is complete.

Hint. Draw the range of whole metres that round to 4 650 m. Where does it overlap your first range?
The planner allows 4 645 through 4 654 m. The app allows 4 650 through 4 749 m. Their overlap is 4 650, 4 651, 4 652, 4 653 and 4 654 m, or 4.650, 4.651, 4.652, 4.653 and 4.654 km.
4–9 min. Laat de andere leerling het tweede bereik tekenen. Laat duo’s elkaars eerste en laatste waarde controleren.

Can Maya trust her shortcut?

“The planner shows 4 650 m. That is 4.65 km, which rounds to 4.7 km. So whenever the planner shows 4 650 m, the app must show 4.7 km.”

Find a counterexample. Then find every exact distance for which her shortcut gives the wrong app reading.

Hint. Try an exact distance just below 4 650 m. Start with that same distance for both methods.
4 645 to 4 649 m. For example, 4.649 km rounds directly to 4.6 km. Maya first rounds 4 649 m to 4 650 m. That becomes 4.65 km, which then rounds to 4.7 km.
9–15 min. Laat twee duo’s met dezelfde afstand elk één methode tonen. Vraag bij welke stap informatie verloren gaat.

Can you leave exactly one answer?

Keep the app display at 4.7 km. Change the planner’s display, keeping its rule of rounding to the nearest ten metres.

Can you make exactly one whole-metre distance fit? Find a display that works or prove none can.

Hint. Try a planner reading near an end of the app’s range, then try one in the middle.
Impossible. Readings of 4 650 m and 4 750 m each leave five possibilities. Each reading from 4 660 m to 4 740 m, in steps of ten, leaves ten possibilities. All other readings leave zero.
Laat snelle duo’s uitleggen waarom een afleeswaarde van de routeplanner altijd een veelvoud van tien is.
