Word formulas

Build, use and explain a word formula.

Who is standing in the circle?

5 min · Work in pairs. Imagine people in the circle playing the roles of beavers and kangaroos. There are exactly three beavers, and no two beavers stand next to each other. Exactly three kangaroos have at least one kangaroo next to them. How many kangaroos are in the circle?

Sketch a circle together or try an arrangement below. In the model, tap a place to switch it between K (kangaroo) and B (beaver).

Use a formula forwards and backwards

8 min · Swap writer. Explain each step to your partner before you check your answer. A word formula uses words to name the quantities. To work forwards, substitute the given number and multiply before you add or subtract. To work backwards, undo the operations in reverse order.

Taxi
Fare in euros = 5 + 2 × distance in kilometres
Candle
Length in cm = 48 − 6 × number of burn hours

Compare two word formulas

6 min · Swap writer again. Draw a short table or compare how much each runner starts with and how much they gain per lap. Agree on one explanation you can share with the class.

Carlijn starts with €12 and receives another €2 per lap. Write her word formula on your board.

Explain your method

3 min. Each partner explains one step aloud. Then agree on your answer and check it together.

Teacher notes

Plan: 5 min circle puzzle, 8 min matchsticks, 8 min taxi and candle, 6 min sponsored run, 3 min exit check. Set up random pairs at vertical whiteboards. If a pair is still on the puzzle after five minutes, prompt them to use the hints. The puzzle stays locked until they submit the correct answer. Optional tasks fit inside the time for that stage.
