From table to graph

Choose a scale, plot points and explain what a graph shows.

Read a walk from left to right

5 min · Think on your own, then compare with a partner. Tess is walking in the mountains. The graph shows how her altitude changes over time.

Turn a table into a graph

8 min · Discuss what each axis should show before looking at the worked example.

  1. Put the variable from the top row on the horizontal axis.
  2. Put the variable from the bottom row on the vertical axis. Choose a scale that fits the largest value. Equal distances on an axis represent equal steps wherever the scale is continuous.
  3. Write a title and label both axes. Include units where needed.
  4. Plot the points. For measurements that change gradually, such as weight over time, draw a smooth curve through them.
Example: At 0, 1, 2 and 3 weeks, a puppy weighs 2, 3, 5 and 6 kg. The point (2, 5) means that the puppy weighs 5 kg at 2 weeks.

Plot Otto’s weight

19 min · Use the measurements to plot all eleven points. Click or tap the grid, or choose a day and type its weight below the graph. Then copy your graph onto squared paper and draw a smooth curve through the points before revealing the model.
