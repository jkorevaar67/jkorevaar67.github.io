3.5 Formulas with letters

Learning goals: I can calculate using mathematical expressions and formulas with letters.

LearnExerciseTest

What does a letter stand for?

In the rental formula r = 6 + 5h, r is the price in euros and h is the number of hours. The letters make the formula shorter. Their meanings and units tell you how to use it.

5h means 5 × h. For h = 3, write the multiplication sign again: r = 6 + 5 × 3 = €21.

Check: What does 4 hours cost?6 + 5 × 4 = €26.

Substitute negative numbers and use brackets

Evaluate 8 − 3(2 − b) for b = 5. Calculate the bracket first: 2 − 5 = −3. Then 8 − 3 × (−3) = 8 + 9 = 17.

In a fraction, work out the numerator and denominator separately. For (2x + 1)/(y − 1) with x = −2 and y = 5, the result is (−4 + 1) ÷ (5 − 1) = −3/4.

Check: What is 5 − 4a when a = −3?5 − 4 × (−3) = 17.

Use a formula with several letters

Let p = 11 + 4a + 2c be a nightly price in euros for a adults and c children. If there are two adults, substitute a = 2 and combine the fixed amounts: p = 19 + 2c.

If the group paid €29, work backwards: 29 − 19 = 10 euros in children's charges and 10 ÷ 2 = 5 children.

Check: If there are three adults, how does the formula change?p = 11 + 4 × 3 + 2c = 23 + 2c.
