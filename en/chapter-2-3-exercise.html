2.3 · Fractions

To add or subtract fractions, use a common denominator. To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators and the denominators.

1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4

Keep the denominator and add the numerators.

1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6

Change both fractions to sixths: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Write fraction answers like 3/4 or -1/2. You may also type a decimal with a dot.
