Theory A

A point has an address

The horizontal line is the x-axis. The vertical line is the y-axis. They meet at the origin O = (0, 0).

Coordinates are always written (x, y). Start at O, move horizontally first, then vertically.

A(3, 1): 3 right, then 1 up.
B(−2, 3): 2 left, then 3 up.
C(−3, −2): 3 left, then 2 down.

Draw your own coordinate plane

  1. Choose where the origin should be on graph paper.
  2. Draw perpendicular horizontal and vertical axes.
  3. Label O, x and y.
  4. Add numbers with equal steps along each axis. Check the scale before plotting.

For this section, one grid square represents one unit.

Theory B

Four quadrants

Number the quadrants anticlockwise, starting at the top right.

Quadrantxy
I · top right++
II · top left−+
III · bottom left−−
IV · bottom right+−
Axes belong to no quadrant. On the x-axis, y = 0. On the y-axis, x = 0. The origin lies on both axes.
Intermediate

Move points and complete shapes

Moving right increases x; moving left decreases x. Moving up increases y; moving down decreases y.

Start at (−3, 2), move 5 right and 4 down:
x = −3 + 5 = 2 and y = 2 − 4 = −2.
The new point is (2, −2).

Horizontal sides have equal y-coordinates; vertical sides have equal x-coordinates. For a rectangle with consecutive vertices A(−2, −1), B(3, −1), C(3, 2), the fourth vertex is D(−2, 2).

Challenge

Between the grid lines

A point such as (1½, −2) lies halfway between two vertical grid lines. In these unit grids, a grid point has two integer coordinates. So (1½, −2) is not a grid point.

Where do diagonals meet?

The diagonals of a rectangle or square meet halfway between opposite vertices. Find the middle of the x-values and the middle of the y-values.

Opposite vertices (−2, 1) and (3, 4):
x = (−2 + 3) ÷ 2 = 0.5
y = (1 + 4) ÷ 2 = 2.5
The centre is (0.5, 2.5), which is not a grid point.

A tilted square

A quarter-turn changes a side’s direction by 90°. If A(−1, −2) to B(2, −1) moves 3 right and 1 up, a left quarter-turn moves 1 left and 3 up. This gives C(1, 2) and D(−2, 1). All four sides have the same length.
